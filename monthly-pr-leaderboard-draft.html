📋 PR Challenge Rules

What counts as a PR? A Personal Record is counted when you beat your previous verified best time by at least 1 second.

How are PRs tracked? The system automatically tracks your best verified time. Each time you submit a new verified time that beats your previous best, it counts as 1 PR.

Verification required: Only verified submissions count toward PR totals. Self-reported times are not accepted.

Tiebreaker: In case of a tie, the athlete with the highest single PR (largest time improvement) wins.

Prize: $200 awarded to the athlete with the most PRs at the end of the month.
