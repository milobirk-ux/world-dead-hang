# Official World Dead Hang Championship (WDHC) Rulebook
**Version 1.0**

## 1. The Bar
- **Standardization:** Must be a standard, smooth cylindrical pull-up bar.
- **Diameter:** Maximum 1.5 inches (38mm). Minimum 1 inch (25mm). No fat bars or specialized grip implements allowed in the standard division.
- **Surface:** Bare metal or powder-coated. Tape, knurling, or foam grips are strictly prohibited.

## 2. The Grip
- **Grip Style:** Both Overhand (Pronated) and Underhand (Supinated) grips are allowed. Hook grip and suicide (thumbless) grip are permitted.
- **Hands:** Both hands must remain on the bar at all times. Re-gripping (momentarily lifting fingers to adjust) is allowed as long as contact with the bar is never fully broken by either hand.
- **Chalk:** Liquid or powder magnesium carbonate chalk is permitted and encouraged. No grip shirts, lifting straps, hooks, or sticky adhesives (pine tar, stickum).

## 3. The Hang
- **Start:** Time begins the moment the athlete's feet completely leave the floor/box and full body weight is suspended by the hands.
- **Position:** Arms must be fully extended. Passive and active hangs are both legal. 
- **Lower Body:** Feet must not touch the floor, walls, or any supporting structure. Crossing the legs at the ankles is permitted.
- **Stop:** Time ends the exact frame either hand completely detaches from the bar or the feet touch the ground.

## 4. Video Submission Standards
To qualify for the global leaderboard, video evidence must be strictly unedited and adhere to the following:
1. **The Setup:** The video must start by showing the bar being used (a close-up to prove no adhesives/tape).
2. **The Frame:** The athlete's entire body (hands on the bar down to the feet) must remain in the video frame for the entire duration of the hang. 
3. **The Timer:** A continuous digital timer must be visible in the frame (either a physical clock in the background or a digitally overlaid, unedited stopwatch).
4. **No Cuts:** Any edits, cuts, or artificial speed adjustments will result in an immediate ban from the leaderboard.

## 5. Weight Classes (Proposed Phase 2)
*(For Phase 1, the leaderboard is Absolute/Open. Phase 2 will introduce the following categories based on weigh-in videos)*
- Lightweight: Under 165 lbs (M) / Under 135 lbs (F)
- Middleweight: 165 - 199 lbs (M) / 135 - 165 lbs (F)
- Heavyweight: 200+ lbs (M) / 165+ lbs (F)
