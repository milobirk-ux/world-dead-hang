# World Dead Hang Championship (WDHC) - Record Holder Agreement

**This is a lightweight agreement confirming the terms of holding an official WDHC world record.**

---

### 1. Parties

This Record Holder Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of **[Date]** by and between:

- **World Dead Hang Championship** ("WDHC"), and
- **[Athlete Name]** ("Record Holder").

### 2. Record Acknowledged

WDHC hereby officially recognizes the Record Holder's performance of **[Time]** in the **[Weight Class/Category]** as the official World Record.

### 3. Record Holder's Agreement

By signing this Agreement, the Record Holder agrees to the following terms for the duration their record is officially recognized by WDHC:

1.  **Exclusivity of Record Claim:** The Record Holder agrees not to simultaneously claim the same record performance under any other competing organization, league, or federation.
2.  **Right to Publicize:** The Record Holder grants WDHC the full right to announce, publicize, and promote the record through any and all media channels.
3.  **Notification of New Records:** The Record Holder agrees to notify WDHC in writing if they subsequently break their own record under different standards or with a competing organization.

This is to maintain the integrity and singularity of the World Record as certified by the World Dead Hang Championship.
