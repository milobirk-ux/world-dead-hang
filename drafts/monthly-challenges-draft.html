📋 Monthly Challenge Rules
📅
Monthly Reset
Challenges reset on the 1st of each month at 12:00 AM UTC. All submissions must be verified during the challenge month.
✅
Verification Required
All submissions must follow standard WDHC verification process with video evidence. Self-reported times are not accepted.
🏆
Prize Distribution
Prizes awarded within 7 days of month end. Winners must provide payment information for prize distribution.
📊
PR Tracking
Personal Records (PRs) tracked automatically. A PR is counted when you beat your previous verified best time.
👥
One Entry Per Category
Athletes can compete in multiple categories but only one entry per category counts toward monthly challenge.
⚖️
Fair Competition
All athletes must follow standard dead hang form rules. Any violation results in disqualification from monthly challenge.
