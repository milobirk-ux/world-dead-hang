WDHC Pro Division

The Highest Tier of Grip Endurance Athletes

Become the world's first Professional Dead Hang Athlete. Earn your WDHC Pro status by reaching the Freak tier and meeting the time threshold for your weight class. No invitation needed — earn your place through performance.

Pro Qualification Times

Reach the Freak tier and hit these times to qualify for Pro Division. These are the minimum thresholds — faster times mean higher rankings.

How to Qualify

1

Reach Freak Tier

Build your way up through Contender → Challenger → Contender Elite → Freak by submitting verified hang times.

2

Hit Your Threshold

Once in Freak tier, achieve the minimum time for your weight class on a verified submission.

3

Automatic Promotion

Qualifying submissions are automatically flagged. Our team reviews and promotes eligible athletes to Pro.

4

Compete Among the Elite

Join the Pro leaderboard and compete for rankings against the best grip athletes in the world.
